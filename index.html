About June on the Moon

A folk poetic voice blending ethereal melodies with grounded storytelling, June on the Moon is a self taught singer, songwriter, multi instrumentalist, guitarist, and producer rooted in Kenya and the Caribbean. Her work moves between jazz, neo soul, bossa nova, R&B, folk, and healing music, drawing from artists such as Nina Simone, Ella Fitzgerald, Amy Winehouse, and Astrud Gilberto while remaining distinctly her own. Her writing explores the social and political realities we carry, nature, the universe, and the experience of being human. Her music is made to be felt first and understood after. Over the past two years, June has toured and collaborated across Accra, Mombasa, Lamu, Kilifi, and Dar es Salaam, including with Folk n Ether, a duo turned four piece band. Through Sessions on the Moon, her live and electronic music platform, she creates intentional spaces where sound, ceremony, and audience become part of the same experience. Now based in Zambia, June is expanding her practice into curation, collaboration, cinematic sound design, and composition for film. She is interested in the relationship between sound, story, and space, bringing her ceremonial practice and sonic architecture into new contexts where music becomes environment, and environment becomes medicine.

About Brown Lotus Records

Brown Lotus Records is dedicated to folk innovation and spiritual expression. Brown Lotus amplifies contemporary voices that expand traditional folk, jazz and RnB sounds into new realms — dream-folk, experimental, and cross-cultural collaborations.

Sessions on the Moon is a Folk and Blues club that curates acoustic music experiences for wanderers who enjoy soul music.

The label’s vision is to create a sacred space for artists who blur boundaries between roots and imagination, carrying folk into the future while holding space for its deep histories.
